20 Stock Lab — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 20 Stock Lab ("20 Stock Lab," "we," "us," or "our") may collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit or use our website, dashboard, software, and related services (the "Service").

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Account information: information you provide to register, sign in, or contact us, such as your email address and account identifiers.
  • App and simulation information: paper-trading settings, watchlists, simulated orders, positions, balances, trade history, and preferences you create or use in the Service.
  • Device and usage information: browser and device details, IP address, diagnostic records, pages or features used, timestamps, and error or security logs.

Please do not send us passwords, secret API keys, payment card details, or other sensitive credentials by email.

We may use information to provide and maintain the Service; create and secure accounts; operate paper-trading and dashboard features; remember settings; troubleshoot errors; prevent abuse; respond to support requests; communicate important service or policy changes; and comply with legal obligations.

The Service may rely on third-party providers for hosting, database, authentication, infrastructure, analytics or diagnostics, and market data. Those providers may process information as needed to provide their services to us. Their own privacy policies and terms also apply to their services. Market information may be supplied by third parties and may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate.

4. Brokerage Connections

As of the effective date of this Policy, 20 Stock Lab is intended for paper trading and does not provide an active live-brokerage connection or place live securities orders. Do not submit brokerage passwords or API secrets to the Service or to us by email. If brokerage-connect or live-trading features are introduced, we will update this Policy to explain the information involved and the applicable providers and controls before those features are made available.
